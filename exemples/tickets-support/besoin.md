# Need: triage of support tickets (fictional case)

A small support team of the fictional company "Orion Pianos" receives about 200 tickets a day.
A small model must read one ticket and answer one of three closed decisions:

| Decision | Meaning | Example (invented) |
|---|---|---|
| `URGENT` | someone must act within the hour | "Order 4471 was charged twice and the customer's account is blocked." |
| `A_VERIFIER` | a person must look before anything is decided | "I think the manual I received is for another model." |
| `RIEN` | nothing to do (thanks, newsletter, duplicate) | "Thanks, the tuning kit arrived, all good." |

Success is measured on an exam of tickets the model has never seen.
